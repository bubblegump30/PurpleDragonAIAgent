# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Purple Dragon AI Agent welcomes respectful participation regardless of experience, disability, age, race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender identity, sexual orientation or background. This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other project-managed community spaces.

## Expected behavior

Be considerate, constructive and specific. Critique code and ideas without attacking people. Welcome newcomers, respect different needs and acknowledge mistakes. Respect privacy, consent, intellectual property and maintainers' time.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, personal attacks, impersonation, deliberate disruption and sharing another person's private information without consent are not acceptable. Do not post credentials, private project content or exploit details in public discussions. Disagreement is welcome; abuse is not.

## Reporting

Contact the repository owner, **@bubblegump30**, through a private project contact channel when one is available. If no private channel is listed, open a brief issue asking the maintainer to arrange private contact; do not include personal details or allegations in that public request. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not publish other people's private information as evidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove or decline content that violates this policy and may warn, temporarily restrict or block participation. They will consider context, severity and repeated behavior, and avoid disclosing reports or identifying information unnecessarily. No response time or confidentiality guarantee is implied by a public GitHub issue. A participant may request review of a moderation decision through the same contact route without restarting the dispute in unrelated threads.

This is a project-specific community policy, maintained by KyleAustin85.
